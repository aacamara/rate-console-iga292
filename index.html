What a floor is

The minimum nightly rate a destination sets around its events. Once a named person approves one, the Rate Desk never recommends a rate under it.

Read it right

  • Simulated: the Rate Desk engine priced each night without and with the draft. Nothing real changes.
  • Live: today's price on brnxtravel.com, the price PriceLabs pushes.
  • Listings: asking prices from the Oct 23 Airbnb sweep (5 to 7 night search). Not booked prices, not revenue.
  • Revenue at stake: the floor minus today's live price over open nights under it, if every one sold. Not a forecast.
  • A window counts every night from its first to its last date, both included.
  • Maui basis: the median comp-set p25 over 36 unit-nights with a market band (US$250.48), rounded down to US$10.
  • Kept by Aziz Camara, aprèsvente. Approved by nobody; no DMO has set it. Event dates estimated (dhrd.hawaii.gov, mauiinvitational.com).
  • Owners still win: mixed, an inference from the market band, not observed bookings.
